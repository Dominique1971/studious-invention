## Procedurally Generated Assets

All audio files stored under `assets/audio/` are generated at runtime by `audio.py` using NumPy. They contain no third-party content and are released under the project's MIT licence.

All GLSL shader source files under `assets/shaders/` are original works released under the project's MIT licence.
